• Newspaper hiện hành & Database: Hall J (24 June 2022) ‘It’s time for a change’, Herald Sun, accessed 4 July 2022, Factiva database.
• Book không tác giả & In-text nghiêng: A Warning (2019) Little Brown, London. → In-text: (A Warning 2019).
• Webpage Homepage & Tên miền URL: Telstra (n.d.) Homepage, Telstra website... (chữ thẳng) & vic.gov.au (không lặp chữ website).
